# Institute Time Clauses (Hulls) & Underwriting Risk Guidelines

## 1. Vessel Age and Condition Guidelines
- **Age < 15 Years**: Standard acceptance. Vessel eligible for standard market terms and baseline premium rates.
- **Age 15 - 20 Years**: Increased risk. Underwriter must review latest classification society survey reports. 15% - 25% premium surcharge applies.
- **Age 20 - 25 Years**: High risk. Mandatory Condition & Structural Survey by an approved marine surveyor before inception. Warranted IACS (International Association of Classification Societies) class maintained without outstanding recommendations.
- **Age > 25 Years**: Strict decline for general cargo and tankers unless specifically approved by Head of Underwriting with 50%+ deductible increase.

## 2. Flag State Assessment (Paris & Tokyo MoU)
- **White List Flags** (e.g. Panama, Liberia, Marshall Islands, Singapore, UK, Bahamas): Normal risk profile.
- **Grey List Flags**: Subject to elevated port state control inspections. Apply 10% premium loading.
- **Black List Flags / Flags of Convenience with Poor Port State Control records**: Automatic referral to senior underwriter. Warranted no single-voyage detentions in prior 12 months.

## 3. Valuation and Sum Insured
- Hull and Machinery (H&M) agreed value must not exceed 115% of current market fair value without independent valuation survey.
- P&I (Protection and Indemnity) liability limit standard maximum: $100,000,000 without dedicated reinsurance treaty endorsement.
