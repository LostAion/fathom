# Institute Cargo Clauses (A, B, C) & Cargo Risk Standards

## 1. Cargo Classification & Risk Profiles
- **General Dry Containerized Cargo**: Low to moderate risk. Standard Institute Cargo Clauses (A) 'All Risks' coverage permitted.
- **High-Value Consumer Electronics & Pharmaceuticals**: High theft risk. Warranted GPS container tracking seals and specialized security escorts during land transit. Deductible minimum $10,000.
- **Reefer / Temperature-Controlled Cargo (Perishables, Frozen Food)**: High spoilage risk. Warranted temperature logging every 4 hours. Coverage subject to 24-hour breakdown of refrigeration machinery clause.
- **Bulk Commodities (Grain, Fertilizer, Coal)**: Susceptible to moisture, shifting, and spontaneous combustion. Moisture certificate required prior to loading.
- **Crude Oil & Refined Petroleum**: High pollution and environmental liability exposure. Vessel must possess valid Civil Liability Convention (CLC) certificate.

## 2. Packaging & Stowage Warranties
- Warranted packed in seaworthy export packaging conforming to IMDG (International Maritime Dangerous Goods) Code where applicable.
- Deck cargo: If carried on deck, coverage is restricted to Institute Cargo Clauses (C) with Jettison & Washing Overboard clause unless shipped in enclosed ISO containers.
