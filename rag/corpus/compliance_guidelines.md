# Underwriting Compliance, Sanctions & Anti-Fraud Standards

## 1. Sanctions Due Diligence (OFAC, UN, EU, UK OFSI)
- **Zero Tolerance Policy**: Any vessel, shipowner, charterer, cargo shipper, consignee, or financial broker appearing on the US OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List, UK OFSI Consolidated List, or EU Financial Sanctions is **STRICTLY DECLINED**.
- **Sanctions Limitation and Exclusion Clause (LMA3100)**: Mandatory clause inserted into every marine policy. No insurer shall provide cover or pay any claim that exposes them to sanction, prohibition, or restriction.
- **Deceptive Shipping Practices (Dark Fleet)**:
  - Intentional disabling or manipulation of AIS (Automatic Identification System) without legitimate safety rationale is a breach of warranty.
  - Frequent mid-ocean ship-to-ship (STS) cargo transfers or flag hopping (multiple flag changes within 24 months) triggers immediate compliance investigation.

## 2. Loss Ratio & Past Claims Thresholds
- **Loss Ratio (LR)** = `Total Incurred Claims / Total Earned Premium`:
  - **LR < 40%**: Excellent underwriting record. Eligible for renewal discounts and expedited approval.
  - **LR 40% - 70%**: Normal industry performance. Standard rating.
  - **LR 70% - 100%**: Elevated loss experience. Underwriter must apply minimum 15% rate increase and review retention limits.
  - **LR > 100%**: Unprofitable account. Refer to Underwriting Committee. Mandatory deductible doubling or terms restriction.
- **Repeat Total Loss / Major Casualty**: If client or vessel had 2 or more major structural claims within 36 months, decline hull coverage unless independent safety audit passed.
