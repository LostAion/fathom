# Joint War Committee (JWC) Listed Areas & Geopolitical Risk Guidelines

## 1. JWC Listed High Risk Maritime Areas (JWLA-032)
The Joint War Committee (JWC) of the Lloyd's Market Association periodically designates marine areas of enhanced risk due to war, piracy, terrorism, and civil unrest:
- **Southern Red Sea and Gulf of Aden (Bab-el-Mandeb)**: High missile and drone attack hazard against commercial shipping. Transits require 48-hour prior notification, armed security escort evaluation, and additional war risk premium (AWRP) of 0.20% - 0.75% of insured vessel value per transit.
- **Black Sea and Sea of Azov**: Active armed conflict zone. Commercial navigation strictly restricted; standard policies automatically exclude navigation in these waters unless specific breach warranty endorsed.
- **Persian Gulf and Strait of Hormuz**: Heightened geopolitical seizure/boarding risk. Navigational limit warranty required.
- **Gulf of Guinea (Offshore Nigeria, Togo, Benin)**: High maritime piracy and kidnapping risk. Best Management Practice (BMP West Africa) compliance warranted.

## 2. Navigational Limits Warranty (Hull & Cargo)
- Every policy incorporates standard Institute Navigational Limits (INL).
- If vessel or cargo voyage breaches INL or enters JWC Listed Areas without written agreement from underwriter prior to departure:
  1. Automatic policy suspension of war/perils coverage OR
  2. Mandatory calculation of Additional War Risk Premium (AWRP) and submission of AIS tracking verification.
